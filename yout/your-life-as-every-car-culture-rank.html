Main Idea

Three Roads. One Obsession.

Inspired by Chill Pov Guy's "Your Life as Every Rank" series — three parallel journeys through car culture.

🔧
Mechanic Path

Fix & Diagnose

From shop porter to shop owner. You learn that understanding the machine — hands inside the system — is the real skill. Lab scope first. Wiring diagrams before parts.

🔥
Tuner Path

Modify & Build

From a rusty Civic in your parents' garage to world-record builds. The car and the driver evolve together. Every mistake becomes knowledge.

🏆
Collector Path

Own & Preserve

From a Ferrari poster on the ceiling to coach-built Italian legends. Stewardship, patience, and knowledge matter more than money.
