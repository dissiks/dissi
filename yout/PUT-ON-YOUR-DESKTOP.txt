# PUT THIS FOLDER ON YOUR DESKTOP

Everything for **Your Life as Every Car Culture Rank** is inside `yout/`.

## Quick steps (Mac or Windows)

### 1. Download
Get the folder from GitHub:
https://github.com/dissiks/dissi/tree/cursor/car-culture-production-html-8d03/yout

Click **Code** → **Download ZIP**

### 2. Move to Desktop
- Unzip the downloaded file
- Drag the **`yout`** folder onto your **Desktop**

### 3. Done!
You should have:

```
Desktop/
└── yout/
    ├── your-life-as-every-car-culture-rank.html   ← open in browser
    ├── your-life-as-every-car-culture-rank.mp4    ← watch video
    └── video-production/
        └── output/
            └── your-life-as-every-car-culture-rank.mp4
```

---

## What each file does

| File | Use |
|------|-----|
| `.html` | Full script, 42 photo prompts, 42 video prompts |
| `.mp4` | Final 7-minute video |
| `build_video.py` | Rebuild the video anytime |
| `scenes.json` | All scene data |

---

**Note:** Files are built in the cloud. Your Desktop is on your computer — you must download once to get them there.
